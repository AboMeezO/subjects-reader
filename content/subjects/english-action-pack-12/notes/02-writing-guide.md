# Action Pack 12 - Writing Guide

## Discursive essay
Discuss both sides. Use contrast and consequence linkers: however, whereas, on the one hand, on the other hand, therefore, as a result.

## Report
Use factual tone, title, introduction, findings, conclusion/recommendations. Useful openings: The aim of this report is to..., This report examines...

## Descriptive essay
Describe a person, place, invention, future situation, or event with organized details. Use clear paragraphs and precise adjectives.

## Summary
Keep main ideas only. Remove examples, repeated ideas, and minor details. Do not add opinion.

## Argumentative essay
Take a clear position. Give reasons, examples, counterargument, and conclusion. Arrange arguments logically.

## Blog post
More personal than an essay, but still organized. Use a clear topic, personal opinion, and direct reader-friendly style.

## Persuasive letter
State the problem, explain consequences, propose solution, and request action politely.

## Competition entry
Answer the prompt exactly. Use engaging language and clear reasons.

## Review
Describe the event/place/book/service, evaluate positives and negatives, then give a recommendation.

## CV and covering letter
CV: personal details, education, experience, skills, references. Covering letter: job applied for, why you fit, relevant experience, polite closing.
