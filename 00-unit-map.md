# Action Pack 12 - Unit Map

The Student's Book scope and sequence divides the course into ten units:

- Unit 1: Information technology - tense revision, passive revision, reported speech - discursive essay.
- Unit 2: A healthy life - be used to, used to, past perfect continuous - report.
- Unit 3: Medical advances - future continuous, future perfect - descriptive essay.
- Unit 4: Success stories - cleft sentences, relative clauses - summary.
- Unit 5: The arts - articles, American English - argumentative essay.
- Unit 6: Education today - comparison quantifiers - blog post.
- Unit 7: Lifelong learning - indirect questions, impersonal passive - persuasive letter.
- Unit 8: Language - phrasal verbs, question tags, passive revision - competition entry.
- Unit 9: The world of business - unreal past forms for regrets and wishes - review.
- Unit 10: Career choices - zero, first, third conditionals - CV and covering letter.
